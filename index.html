🛡️ Our Unique Feature

The Nova Safe Marketplace
with Escrow Protection

The problem: Nigerian online buyers constantly fear being defrauded. Our solution: When you buy through Nova, we hold your money safely until you confirm you received exactly what you ordered. No more "what I ordered vs. what I got." Forever.

01

Buyer Places Order

Customer browses the Nova Marketplace, selects an item, and completes the checkout. Payment is collected from their wallet.

02

Nova Holds Payment

The full payment is locked securely in Nova's escrow vault. The seller receives a notification to ship or deliver the item. Money is NOT released yet.

03

Item Delivered

Seller ships/delivers the item. Buyer has 7 days to inspect it. If there is an issue, they raise a dispute. Nova's team investigates and resolves fairly.

04

Funds Released

Buyer confirms receipt and satisfaction. Nova immediately releases the full payment to the seller's account. Both parties are protected. Always.
